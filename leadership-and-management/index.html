From team leader to manager: what the Diploma of Leadership and Management covers

There is a moment in many careers when you stop being judged mainly on your own work and start being judged on how your team performs. Some people get there through a formal promotion. Others drift into it because they were the one who knew how things worked. Either way, the skills that made you good at your old job are not quite the same skills a manager needs.

The BSB50420 Diploma of Leadership and Management is written for that shift. Training.gov.au describes it as a qualification for people who lead and manage across a range of industries, plan and organise their own work and the work of others, and solve problems that are not always predictable. It is an AQF Level 5 qualification.

Six core units, six management habits

Every student completes six core units. Each lines up with something managers are expected to do well:

Influence. BSBCMM511 Communicate with influence covers negotiating, leading meetings and presenting your case to stakeholders.

Thinking skills in others. BSBCRT511 Develop critical thinking in others is about coaching your team to think critically and creatively, rather than solving every problem yourself.

Relationships. BSBLDR523 Lead and manage effective workplace relationships covers setting up processes that support working relationships and resolving issues across a team.

Planning. BSBOPS502 Manage business operational plans asks you to build an operational plan, manage resources and monitor performance against key indicators.

Self-awareness. BSBPEF502 Develop and use emotional intelligence focuses on self-awareness, self-management and helping others build emotional intelligence at work.

Team performance. BSBTWK502 Manage team effectiveness covers leading a team to perform well, building a positive culture and working with stakeholders across the organisation.

Taken together, these describe a manager who plans, communicates, coaches and keeps relationships healthy, which is a fair summary of the job.

Electives you choose

You then pick six electives. 3CIR Online offers 18 electives to choose from, grouped around leading people, operations and finance, and communication. Examples include BSBFIN501 Manage budgets and financial plans and BSBHRM522 Manage employee and industrial relations. That flexibility lets a team leader in a service business and a supervisor in an operations setting each build a course that reflects their own role.

Who it suits

The course page lists a few situations where it fits well:

  • You supervise people now and want a qualification that matches the role.
  • You are a team leader aiming for a manager position.
  • You run a small business or team and want a formal grounding in managing it.
  • You are ready to commit to a Diploma level workload alongside your job.

If you have no experience leading people yet, the Certificate IV in Leadership and Management is suggested as a starting point instead.

How it is studied

The course is online and self-paced, available Australia-wide, and assessed through practical workplace tasks with no exams. For most managers that means using real situations, such as a team plan, a difficult conversation or a meeting you ran, as the basis for assessment.

To enrol you need either a formal qualification or to complete a short entry assessment covering language, literacy, numeracy and digital skills. Enrolment is open to Australian citizens, permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia.

Before you decide

Look at the full unit list and ask whether it describes the manager you are trying to become. If most of it does, the Diploma of Leadership and Management is likely a good match. You can read every core unit and elective on the course page at 3CIR Online.
